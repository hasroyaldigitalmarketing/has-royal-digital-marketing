Who We Are

HAS Royal Digital Marketing is a specialized digital marketing consultancy based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, serving businesses across Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, and surrounding regions.

We specialize in helping businesses in service-based industries - from home appliance repair to real estate, healthcare to hospitality - get more leads, more calls, and more revenue through data-driven digital marketing.

Our approach is simple: we focus on what actually moves the needle for your business. No fluff, no vanity metrics - just real leads and real ROI.
